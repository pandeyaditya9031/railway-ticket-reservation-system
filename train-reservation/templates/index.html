Digitised from a legacy console booking program — now on the web

01 · Train Details

Every train currently in the system, with live seat availability by class.

02 · Update Train Details

Staff only. Add a new train or overwrite an existing train number's details.

Seats per class

03 · Reserve a Ticket

Confirmed if a seat is free in your chosen class, otherwise you're placed on the waitlist.

04 · Cancel a Ticket

Enter your reservation number to cancel. A waitlisted passenger moves up automatically.

05 · Check Ticket Status

Look up a reservation to see the passenger, class and current status.

Rebuilt from a Turbo C++ console project — Flask + SQLite backend, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend.
